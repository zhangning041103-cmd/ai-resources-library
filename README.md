## How to Use This Repository

1. Browse the resource categories.
2. Choose a tool or prompt that matches your needs.
3. Customize prompts with your own goals, context, and constraints.
4. Review AI-generated results and verify important information independently.

## Contributing

Useful contributions are welcome.

You can help improve this repository by suggesting:

- Useful AI tools with official links.
- High-quality prompts with clear objectives and output requirements.
- Practical tutorials and learning resources.
- Corrections, improvements, and examples based on real use.

When suggesting a resource, explain what it does, who may benefit from it, and why it is useful. Please prioritize accuracy, practical value, and clear documentation.

## Responsible Use

AI tools can produce inaccurate, outdated, or misleading information. Verify important claims before relying on them.

Always review the privacy policies, terms of service, and licensing conditions of third-party tools and resources before use.

## Project Status

This is an evolving collection. Resources may be added, revised, or removed as the library develops.

If you find something useful, consider sharing the repository with others who may benefit from it.
