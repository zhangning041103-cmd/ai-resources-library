# AI Prompts Library

A collection of useful AI prompts.

## 1. Writing Assistant

Prompt:
Act as a clear and helpful writing assistant. Help me improve the following text while preserving its original meaning.

## 2. Learning Assistant

Prompt:
Explain the following topic in simple language. Give examples and summarize the key points.

## 3. Idea Generator

Prompt:
Generate 10 practical ideas about the following topic. For each idea, explain its purpose and potential value.

## Usage

Copy a prompt and customize it for your own needs.
